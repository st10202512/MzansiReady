## Full Description (4000 chars max)
MzansiReady is your all-in-one South African daily information app.

Stay prepared with real-time weather, community alerts, and daily
information — all in one place.

KEY FEATURES:
☀️ Live weather from Open-Meteo
🌍 Multi-language support (English + isiZulu)
📶 Full offline mode — works without internet
🔔 Push notifications for important alerts
👤 Secure login with Google Sign-In
🌙 Light and Dark themes
📍 Save multiple locations
📝 Community feed to share with other users

Built for South Africans, by South Africans.

Weather data provided by Open-Meteo (open-meteo.com).
