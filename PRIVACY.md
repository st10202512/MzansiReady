# Privacy Policy — MzansiReady

**Last updated:** 19/10/2026

## Data We Collect
- Email address (for account creation)
- Full name (for personalization)
- Saved locations (for weather)
- Language and theme preferences
- Optional: profile picture (if uploaded)

## How We Use Data
- To display weather for saved locations
- To personalize your app experience
- To send push notifications (if enabled)

## Data Storage
- Passwords: hashed with BCrypt (never stored in plain text)
- Session tokens: stored in EncryptedSharedPreferences (Android Keystore)
- Weather data: cached locally in Room database
- Backend: PostgreSQL database hosted on Supabase

## Third-Party Services
- Open-Meteo (weather data): https://open-meteo.com/en/terms
- Firebase (auth, notifications, storage): https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy
- Supabase (database): https://supabase.com/privacy

## Your Rights
- Delete your account: contact support
- Clear local data: uninstall the app
- Disable notifications: Settings → Notifications
